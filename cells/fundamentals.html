The electrochemical building blocks

In a lithium-ion cell, electrodes store lithium at different chemical potentials. The electrolyte transports ions; the separator keeps the electrodes physically apart while allowing ionic transport. During discharge, electrons travel through the external circuit and ions move internally. Charging reverses these flows.

Three common physical formats

Cylindrical

A round, rigid case. Integration questions include spacing, electrical connections and available cooling surfaces.

Prismatic

A rectangular, rigid case. Check terminal geometry, expansion allowance and the supplier’s mechanical requirements.

Pouch

A flexible laminate enclosure. The surrounding design must address support, tab routing and swelling.

Format is not chemistry

A shape does not identify the electrode materials. Record chemistry and format separately; neither alone determines a cell’s performance.

What do LFP and NMC mean?

LFP refers to lithium iron phosphate; NMC refers to lithium nickel manganese cobalt oxide. These labels describe cathode material families, not a complete performance specification.

What belongs in a cell selection brief?

Document the duty cycle, voltage window, required energy, current profile, mass and volume limits, expected environment, life target and supplier evidence. Compare candidate cells under matching conditions.
