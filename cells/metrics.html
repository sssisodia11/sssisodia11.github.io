SUMIT SINGH SISODIA / BATTERY CELLS

Numbers with context.

Use nominal values for initial estimates, and measured operating conditions for engineering decisions.

Capacity · Ah

Charge delivered under a specified discharge procedure. Record current, temperature and cutoff voltage.

Energy · Wh

Delivered energy is the integral of voltage × current over time. Nominal voltage × capacity gives a first-order estimate.

C-rate · h⁻¹

Current divided by reference capacity. A 5 Ah cell at 2C corresponds to 10 A; it does not guarantee a 30-minute discharge.

Resistance & ageing

Record resistance measurement method and conditions. Capacity retention and resistance growth describe different aspects of ageing.

Cell arithmetic

Editable example inputs, not measured results or operating recommendations.

E ≈ Vnom × Q; specific energy ≈ 1000E / mass(g); I = C-rate × Q. Excludes pack hardware, losses, voltage variation and ageing. C-rate inputs do not establish a cell’s allowable current.
